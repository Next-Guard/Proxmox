## What happens when you visit the site

The site is made of static pages. There are no accounts, forms or comments, and we do not ask for personal data.

- **Hosting and delivery.** The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) and delivered through Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.). To deliver the pages and protect the site, these providers process technical data such as the IP address, browser type and the time of the request, under their own privacy policies.
- **Preferences stored in your browser.** The site remembers your language, the light or dark theme and your cookie choice in the browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is needed for the site to work as you expect, so it does not require consent.

## Statistics cookies (Google Analytics)

With your consent, we use **Google Analytics 4**, through Google Tag Manager, provided by Google Ireland Limited. It tells us which pages are read, how visitors arrive and from which countries, so that we can improve the manual.

- **Nothing is loaded before you choose.** Until you press *Accept*, no Google script runs and no statistics cookie is set.
- **What is collected:** the pages visited, the time spent on them, the referring page, the approximate location (country/region), and the device and browser type. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses.
- **No advertising.** Advertising storage and personalization are always disabled.
- **Cookies:** `_ga` and `_ga_<ID>`, used to distinguish visits. Their duration is set by Google Analytics (up to 2 years).
- **Legal basis:** your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).
- **Data transfers:** Google may process data outside the EU under the safeguards described in its [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

You can change your choice at any time from **Cookie preferences** at the bottom of every page. If you withdraw your consent, the statistics cookies are deleted from this site.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, [garanteprivacy.it](https://www.garanteprivacy.it)).
